## 説明文の案（英語）

```
Learn Japanese one line at a time — from classic Japanese stories.

Each short takes a single sentence from a famous story (Akutagawa, Miyazawa Kenji and more), reads it at natural speed, breaks it into pieces with readings (furigana) and meanings, explains one grammar point, then lets you repeat it.

■ How it works
・Today's line — natural speed, with furigana and English
・Breakdown — word by word, read slowly
・Repeat after me — shadow the line yourself

■ About the stories
All stories are in the public domain (Aozora Bunko), retold in simple Japanese for learners. Scripts are drafted with AI and checked by a native Japanese speaker.

■ Credits
Voice: COEIROINK:おふとんP
Music: original
```

- 声のクレジットは COEIROINK の規約で必要（D-011）。声を足したら書き足す。
- 「AI で下書きし、日本人が確かめている」と書く（docs/policy.md と同じ方針）。
- 収録する作家を増やしたら、1行目の例（Akutagawa, Miyazawa Kenji）を見直す。
